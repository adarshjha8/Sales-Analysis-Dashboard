# Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

##  Project Overview

The **Super Store Sales Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, order volume, shipping methods, customer segments, and regional sales distribution.

The dashboard transforms sales data into actionable insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, trend analysis, and geographical sales analysis. It helps stakeholders understand business performance and identify important sales and profit patterns.


##  Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, order volume, and shipping performance.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends across years.
- Compare sales across regions, customer segments, and payment modes.
- Identify top-performing product categories and subcategories.
- Understand geographical sales distribution across states.
- Enable interactive analysis using regional filters.

##  Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization.

- **DAX** — Calculated measures and KPI calculations, if used in the project.

##  Key Insights

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- **Sales and orders:** The dashboard reports approximately 1.6M in sales and 22K orders.
- **Payment preference:** COD accounts for 43% of sales distribution, followed by Online at 35% and Cards at 22%.
- **Regional contribution:** West contributes 33%, East 29%, Central 22%, and South 16% of the displayed regional distribution.
- **Customer segments:** Consumer is the largest segment at 48%, followed by Corporate at 33% and Home Office at 19%.
- **Shipping performance:** Standard Class has the highest displayed sales value among shipping modes, at approximately 0.33M.
- **Category performance:** Office Supplies leads with approximately 0.64M in sales, followed by Technology at 0.47M and Furniture at 0.45M.
- **Subcategory performance:** Phones leads the displayed subcategories at approximately 0.20M, followed by Chairs at 0.18M and Binders at 0.17M.

*These insights reflect the displayed dashboard values and labels. The payment, regional, and customer-segment percentages are treated as the proportions shown in the visuals.*
